# Brand and voice

Read before writing any copy. These rules sit on top of [copywriting.md](copywriting.md). Where they conflict, this file wins.

## Who's talking

Charlie Barks, publisher of Live Love Las Cruces: a 3x-a-week newsletter with **10,000+ subscribers** covering real events, live music and community in Las Cruces, New Mexico. Always write the count as "10,000+ subscribers".

Write like a friend who always knows where the good stuff is. Warm, casual, community insider. Never marketer-formal.

## Voice rules

- **The town is the hero. Charlie is the connector.** Spotlight the artists, musicians, venues and organizers, not the website.
- **Take a stance.** Never a dry, neutral listing. "Most people sleep on this venue, but it's quietly becoming the best live music room in town" beats "Venue offering live music."
- **Unreasonable hospitality.** Every visitor, local or tourist, should feel like they're getting insider tips from someone who loves this town.
- **Celebrate locals like family.** Generous, specific, never snarky about local people or businesses.
- **Light humor and local references** (green chile, the Organ Mountains, Mesilla Plaza, the Aggies) when they fit. Don't force them into every line.
- **Short and scannable.** Short paragraphs. Every word earns its place.
- **Real, not fluffy.** No corporate filler, no hype words (see the slop list in copywriting.md).
- **No em dashes** (Kevin's rule, adopted here): use commas, colons, periods or parentheses.

## Page copy pattern for listings and events

1. Opinionated hook (why this matters, in one line)
2. The essentials (what, where, when, link) woven in naturally, not as a form
3. A warm nudge to go ("Tell them Live Love Las Cruces sent you")

## Where voice applies

- **Full voice:** headlines, intros, section intros, CTAs, empty states, emails, share text.
- **Plain and clear:** forms, error messages, legal pages, edit and claim flows. Friendly, but clarity first.
- **Never rewrite** an artist's, musician's, venue's or organizer's own description without asking. It's their listing.

## Shared LLLC brand elements

- Cream page background `#FAF9F5` and 20px corner radius match livelovelascruces.com.
- The Live Love Las Cruces heart red `#FF0A06` is decorative only: too light for text on cream.
- Each site has its own palette and fonts in its `SITE.md`. The sites should feel like one family, not identical twins.
- Every site links back to the newsletter. Newsletter links carry `utm_source=<site>` so Beehiiv shows where signups came from.
